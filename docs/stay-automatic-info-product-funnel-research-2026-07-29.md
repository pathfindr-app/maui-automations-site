# Stay Automatic Competitor/Funnel Research — AI for Small Business Info Products

Date: 2026-07-29

## Executive takeaways

The market splits into five price bands:

1. **Free lead magnet / pay-what-you-want**: prompt packs, readiness quizzes, free reports, tool/template libraries. Good for email capture and trust, but often commoditized.
2. **Tripwire $7–$29**: prompt packs, mini playbooks, starter workflows. Impulse-buy zone; heavy use of “save X hours” hooks.
3. **Core DIY guide $47–$197**: deeper blueprint, roadmap, templates, audit worksheets. Good fit for Stay Automatic’s first paid offer if positioned premium/honest.
4. **Course $497–$1,500+**: self-paced or cohort with certificate, office hours, community, worksheets. Trust must be stronger: instructor authority, reviews, guarantees.
5. **Consulting/setup $197 discovery → $2,500–$15,000+ implementation**: paid strategy sessions, readiness assessments, setup packages. Best premium ladder after the guide.

Stay Automatic should avoid the “$27 prompt dump / automate everything while you sleep” lane and own a more credible operator lane: **“Find the 3 workflows worth automating, save real time without replacing your judgment, and know when AI is not the answer.”**

## Common pricing patterns

- **Prompt packs:** $0–$27; Etsy/Gumroad often $2.99–$15; launch “reg. $147 now $27” is common but often feels fake.
- **Workflow playbooks/blueprints:** $7 starter, $24–$79 core, $97 premium bundle.
- **Self-paced certificate courses:** around $497–$500 for role-specific AI courses with certificate and guarantee.
- **Cohort courses:** often waitlist/variable pricing; premium pages lean on ratings, instructor authority, office hours, capstone projects, and community.
- **Readiness/strategy calls:** $197 tripwire is common for small-business scorecard calls; consultant discovery commonly framed at $2,500–$5,000, with deeper assessments $7,000–$35,000+.
- **Implementation/setup:** small-business custom AI automation/platform implementations often quote $10,000–$30,000 when positioned as custom systems; lower-ticket setup packages can sit at $1,500–$5,000 for narrow workflows.

## Common lead magnets

1. **AI readiness quiz / scorecard**: 5–15 questions, score 0–100, personalized roadmap. Strong fit for Stay Automatic.
2. **Time-waste audit worksheet**: calculate hours lost by task and annual dollar leakage.
3. **Workflow priority matrix**: quick wins vs strategic automations; helps avoid “automate everything” hype.
4. **Free prompt pack**: now table stakes; works best as a bonus, not core value.
5. **Template library / starter workflows**: 3–10 specific workflows in Gmail, Drive, Sheets, Calendar, CRM, etc.
6. **Free video/training/resource**: “what’s possible to automate today.” Good for retargeting and warming cold ad traffic.
7. **Course finder quiz**: role/industry-specific routing. Good if Stay Automatic eventually builds vertical pages.

## Landing page patterns that repeat

- **Pain-first hero:** “You didn’t start your business to do admin/bookkeeping/follow-up emails.”
- **Time-back promise:** “save 5–15 hours/week” is the dominant claim.
- **No-code reassurance:** “No technical background,” “copy-paste,” “works with ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini.”
- **Business-function modules:** marketing, sales, customer support, operations/admin, finance, HR.
- **Roadmap structure:** 30/60/90 days or 4-week implementation.
- **Specific deliverables:** prompts, workflows, calculators, templates, ROI estimates, checklists, recordings, certificate.
- **Before/after block:** before = scattered tasks and app-hopping; after = faster replies, scheduled content, automated reminders, organized documents.
- **FAQ objection handling:** tools needed, technical skill, time commitment, business types, refund, certificate.
- **Human approval language:** best SaaS/agent pages now mention review/approval for sensitive actions; this is a trust differentiator.

## Trust signals competitors use

- Star ratings/review counts: Gumroad ratings; Maven 4.6/325 and 4.8/16; Workplace AI testimonials.
- Logos: Workplace AI uses employer/customer logos; Allie Miller uses Mastercard, Slack, Salesforce, Novartis, Samsung.
- Named experience metrics: “100+ leaders,” “126 production systems,” “15+ years enterprise experience,” “4000+ hours coaching.”
- Specificity: page count, module count, number of workflows, exact tools covered.
- Certificates and exams for courses.
- “Last updated” timestamps and lifetime updates.
- Honest disqualification: “what this is not,” “not set-it-and-forget-it,” “skip assessment if you already know your use case.” This aligns strongly with Kyle’s taste.

## Refund / guarantee styles

- **30-day money-back guarantee:** common in self-paced course pages; Workplace AI states “If you don’t see results, full refund. No questions asked.”
- **Maven Guarantee:** refund up to halfway point for cohort courses.
- **Gumroad:** many products do not foreground refund terms; trust relies on low price and platform checkout.
- **Consulting:** fewer refund guarantees; stronger approach is paid diagnostic with explicit deliverable and credit toward implementation.
- **Best Stay Automatic version:** not “guaranteed 15 hours saved,” but “If the guide doesn’t help you identify at least one credible automation opportunity or one thing you should *not* automate, email within 30 days for a refund.” Honest and aligned.

## Pitfalls to avoid

- **Fake urgency and fake discounts:** “$147 value now $7” is everywhere and feels cheap.
- **Prompt-pack positioning as the main product:** prompts are commoditized by Etsy, PromptBase, and OpenAI’s free resources.
- **Overpromising autonomy:** “while you sleep” is compelling but risky if customer messages, finance, legal, or public posting are involved.
- **Generic small-business examples:** every page says marketing/email/social/invoicing. Stay Automatic needs sharper local/operator examples.
- **Tool-first teaching:** buyers do not care about Zapier vs n8n first; they care about fewer missed follow-ups, less app-switching, cleaner handoffs.
- **Free audits that consume human time:** use automated quiz/report for free; charge for Kyle’s judgment.
- **Selling “AI readiness” as the destination:** the score must lead to a paid guide, a roadmap, or a scoped implementation.

## Recommended Stay Automatic funnel

### 1. Ad / hook
Use plain, owner-native creative, not futurist hype.

Example hooks:

- “You don’t need an AI transformation. You need your inbox, calendar, docs, and follow-ups to stop stealing your day.”
- “Find the 3 tasks in your business AI can actually help with — and the ones it should leave alone.”
- “If you’re still switching between Gmail, Drive, Sheets, texts, and calendars all day, take the 3-minute workflow leak quiz.”
- “AI for small business, without the fake ‘make money while you sleep’ stuff.”

### 2. Quiz lead magnet
Name options:

- **The Workflow Leak Quiz**
- **AI Fit Check for Small Business Owners**
- **Stay Automatic Time-Back Scorecard**

Quiz dimensions:

- Weekly hours lost to repetitive admin.
- Where work piles up: inbox, scheduling, follow-up, documents, social, leads, bookkeeping, customer support.
- Current tools: Gmail/Google Workspace, QuickBooks, Square, CRM, Calendly, spreadsheets, POS, booking tools.
- Human-risk level: public/customer/financial/legal decisions.
- Process maturity: “documented,” “mostly in my head,” “changes every week.”
- Owner bandwidth: DIY setup vs wants done-for-you.

Output segments:

1. **Prompt-first:** needs better use of ChatGPT/Claude before automation.
2. **Simple automation:** Zapier/Make/n8n + existing tools can save time.
3. **Agent-ready:** recurring workflows + enough data + clear approval gates.
4. **Not ready yet:** process needs cleanup before AI.

### 3. Tripwire / paid guide
Product: **The Stay Automatic Small Business AI Field Guide**

Recommended price tests:

- **$47** launch/core guide: premium enough to avoid Etsy prompt-pack feel, still low friction from ads.
- **$97** guide + templates + mini video walkthroughs + quiz-generated roadmap export.
- **$197** “Guide + 45-minute Workflow Triage” limited quantity or post-purchase bump.

Core promise:

> “In one afternoon, identify your highest-value workflow, map the tools already involved, write the first AI delegation prompt, and decide whether it should stay manual, become a simple automation, or graduate to an agent with approval gates.”

Include:

- Workflow leak audit.
- 30/60/90 implementation roadmap.
- 12–20 high-quality prompts only as support, not the product.
- Owner approval-gate checklist.
- Tool map for Google Workspace, Sheets, Calendar, QuickBooks, Square/POS, CRM, booking, SMS/email.
- 5 local-business walkthroughs: restaurant/food truck, tour/activity operator, contractor/roofer, professional service office, retail/ecommerce.
- “Do not automate this yet” checklist.

### 4. Order bump
- **$19–$29:** “Local Business Prompt + SOP Pack” or “Workflow Leak Calculator spreadsheet.”
- Must be concrete and non-hype.

### 5. Upsell
- **$197 Workflow Triage Session:** written mini-report + call; credit toward setup.
- **$1,500–$3,500 Setup Sprint:** one narrow workflow implemented with human approvals.
- **$5,000–$12,000 Operating Layer Build:** multi-tool agent desk for serious operators.

### 6. Guarantee
Use a candid guarantee:

> “30-day plain-English guarantee: if this does not help you identify one workflow worth improving — or one automation you should avoid — reply and we’ll refund you. No hoops.”

For consulting:

> “If the triage shows Stay Automatic is not the right fit, we’ll say so and leave you with the roadmap.”

### 7. Landing page structure

1. Hero: “AI for small business owners who want their time back without handing the wheel to a robot.”
2. Quiz CTA above fold.
3. Pain section: app-switching, missed follow-ups, document hunting, repeat questions.
4. Credibility: real Stay Automatic proof/logos/case snippets as available; keep claims conservative.
5. What you get: guide, calculator, workflow maps, approval checklist, templates.
6. “What this is not”: not a prompt dump, not a magic autopilot, not a course that makes you rebuild your business around 20 tools.
7. Segmented outcomes: prompt-first/simple automation/agent-ready/not ready.
8. Price card: $47 or $97 with bonuses.
9. Guarantee.
10. Upsell path: optional workflow triage / setup sprint.

## Best strategic position

Stay Automatic should be **premium relative to prompt packs, cheaper/faster than a course, and more honest than AI automation agencies.** The most credible wedge is not “we teach AI”; it is:

> **Stay Automatic helps busy owners turn scattered app-work into clean, delegated workflows — with AI where it helps, simple automation where that’s better, and human approval where judgment matters.**

This positioning lets Kyle sell a paid guide at $47–$97, an assessment/triage at $197, and real setup work at $1,500+ without sounding like the market’s hype merchants.
