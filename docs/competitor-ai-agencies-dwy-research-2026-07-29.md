## Market pricing pattern

- **DIY/community courses:** free to ~$7/month to ~$184/month; high-ticket upsells $5k–$10k.
- **Managed SMB AI employee/platform:** ~$50/month entry point.
- **Freelancer/DWY mentorship:** ~$2k–$10k.
- **SMB consulting quick win:** ~$2.5k–$15k.
- **Boutique implementation:** ~$8k–$75k+ build, often plus $1.5k–$15k/month retainer.

## White space for Stay Automatic

- **Owner-side, not agency-side:** Most loud competitors teach people to sell AI automation. Stay Automatic should be for people who run businesses and want operational leverage.
- **Open-source/no-lock-in:** Very few offers lead with stack ownership. Make Hermes/open harness ownership central.
- **Transparent setup path:** Competitors often require calls, hidden pricing, or platform lock-in. A clear “from zero to working agent” guide with known costs is differentiated.
- **Pre-consulting wedge:** Position as the rational first step before spending $5k–$25k on consultants.
- **Workflow-first templates:** Avoid generic “AI transformation.” Package around concrete tasks: inbox triage, client research, meeting follow-up, document drafting, CRM updates, local lead response, proposal generation.
- **Candid anti-hype voice:** The market is saturated with “get client #1,” “AI agency in 7 days,” and vague ROI claims. Stay Automatic can win trust by being blunt about what agents can/can’t do.
- **Community of operators:** A practical community for nontechnical professionals running AI in their own companies is less crowded than Skool agency communities.

## Candid pushback for Kyle / Stay Automatic

- The low end is crowded and cheap: free Skool courses and $50/month managed agents will anchor buyers’ expectations.
- A “setup guide” alone may feel too thin unless bundled with templates, walkthroughs, troubleshooting, community, and office hours.
- “Hermes Agent” as the technical core is a strength for ownership but may scare nontechnical buyers unless abstracted as “your own AI command center.”
- Best wedge: **“Set up your own AI agent system in a weekend, own it forever, and automate one real workflow before you hire a consultant.”**
